### Model details (all switches; defaults in brackets)
- Input: (x⁺ without base_xy/base_yaw, ũ, ξ) normalized; `use_xi` [on]. Output: normalized delta over all dims except
  `last_action`. `last_action` of x̂⁻_{t+1} is set to the chosen undelayed u_t (`last_action_source=chosen`; matches
  the env's previous-action obs; `applied` / `predict` available).
- base_xy / base_yaw: never an input (translation/yaw invariance); their delta is predicted in the heading frame of
  x_t and rotated, yaw wrapped. So the world-frame (wm-data) and window-frame conventions both work.
- Loss: mean over windows, k_train steps and predicted dims of ((x̂⁻ − x⁻)/σ_Δ)², σ_Δ = per-dim std of the one-step
  delta (floor 1e-3). **Method A** [default]: at a step with `perturbed_next | dr_resampled_next`, continue from the
  recorded x⁺ (teacher forcing). **Method B** (`--method B`): windows with a shock between two of their steps are
  masked. Ensemble members each roll out on their own predictions; losses are summed.
- Optimizer: Adam 2e-4, global clip 1.0, batch 4096 windows, neg (near-failure) share 0.3, 50 updates per jit call.
- `rollout(wm, bundle, x0, xi, delay_buffer0, u0, policy_fn, H, delay_step_fn, open_loop)`: u0 goes through
  `delay_step` at t, then u_j = policy_fn(x̂_j); returns x̂_{t+1..t+H} (H, N, S). Closed loop [default]: ∇_{u0} also
  flows through the policy's reaction; `open_loop=True` stops the gradient at the policy outputs (same values).
  With wm-data: `delay_step_fn = lambda b, u: wm_state.delay_step(b, u, wm_state.delay_steps_from_xi(xi_full, xl))`,
  `policy_fn = lambda x: policy(full_obs_fn(x, command, xi_full))`, WM ξ = `xi_full[..., meta["schema"]["xi_active"]]`.

### Data (wm-data schema `pncbf-p1-wmdata/v1`)
- Split from `meta.json` `split` (test = seeds 145/245, ppo 1342/1442); without it, `--heldout_pattern`.
- Compact in memory: x⁺_{t+1} = x_plus[t+1] inside a window (not loaded); x⁻_{t+1} stored only where it differs from
  x_plus[t+1] (shock / DR / done rows; the loader checks every row and stores all mismatches); ξ = xi_table[xi_idx],
  only the dims that vary on the training data (`xi_active`, saved in the bundle).
- Windows starting at `episode_step == 0` are masked (stale IMU values after a reset).
- Logged checks: last_action(x⁻_{t+1}) == u_cmd_t; rows where x⁻_{t+1} ≠ x⁺ of the next row outside shock/done rows.

## Training on real data

### Dev shard (wm-data `v1_dev`: ii_h25 seed 42, action noise p=0.4, 256 envs × 1000 steps; job 182838 @ b228926)
512×3, k_train 8, method A, ξ on (263 of 507 ξ dims vary), 20k updates in 9.5 min (~40 updates/s on an A5000),
normalized k=8 loss 1.22 → 0.27. Check: last_action(x⁻_{t+1}) == u_cmd_t exactly on all 254k rows.
In-sample k-step RMSE (model / persistence) at k = 1 / 8 / 25: imu_height 0.015/0.017, 0.0065/0.044,
0.015/0.054 m; joint_pos 0.024/0.092, 0.063/0.34, 0.11/0.37 rad; base_lin_vel 0.036/0.081, 0.085/0.36,
0.15/0.40 m/s. Near failure (last 50 steps), k=25: imu_height 0.018 vs 0.133 m.

**The k=1 error was above the k=8 error for imu_height / roll_pitch / base_yaw. Diagnosis (diag_wm.py, 183105/183108):**
- imu_height: the top 1 % of 1-step windows hold 44 % of the squared error, and they are the transitions right after
  a shock. x⁺ after a shock keeps the pre-shock IMU groups (the env does not recompute them), so the next
  transition's height jump cannot be predicted from that input. Fix (f035112): no window starts at a post-shock
  state (like episode_step 0) and, in method A, no loss on the step after a teacher-forced shock
  (`WMConfig.mask_post_shock_step`, default on).
- roll_pitch: euler roll jumps at ±π when the robot is on its back (errors of 2π − x). Fix: roll is a wrapped
  angle like yaw (`WMLayout.angle_idx`).
- base_yaw: 93 % of the 1-step yaw error comes from states with |pitch| ≈ π/2 (gimbal lock, fallen robot); not fixed,
  harmless for the height margin.
The dev numbers above are from before these fixes.

**bf16 forward** (same trained params, bf16 matmuls with f32 accumulation, 183106): every group's k-step RMSE is
within 1 % of the f32 forward (e.g. k=25 imu_height 0.01525 vs 0.01522 m). bf16 costs no accuracy.

**Gate check on the dev shard** (gate_wm.py, 183107; 100 steps × 256 envs; V from the H=25 imagination with the
checkpoint's policy, γ_c = 0 logit = p_c 0.5 as a rough stand-in, T_ℓ = 0.1): 2.2 % of states have V < 0 (actual
25-step failure rate 2.3 %); none of them is outside the top 25 % in any step (0 % missed for δ = 0/1/2; no step
with more than 25 % of its envs needing a correction). With T_ℓ = 0.1 and the 0.05 m height scale, V is nearly
binary (saturates at +27.6 for safe states, ≈ −590 on average for failing ones), so the band δ hardly matters.
The held-out run reports the per-step maximum and the AUC of V too.

**Margin temperature (gate_wm.py v2, local CPU test on a 20-step × 64-env cut of the dev shard, in-sample WM — too
few states for numbers, only the pattern).** margin_fall is min(height margin / 0.05 m, qvel margin / 10 m/s); at
eval the height threshold is 0, so ℓ ≈ IMU height in units of 5 cm (≈ 7 for a standing Go2), not normalized by h_nom.
- T_ℓ = 0.1 and 0.3: V saturated (binary); ∇_u V = 0 / 0.5 away from the boundary, no state with |V| < 2.
- T_ℓ = 1: Brier 0.0020 (constant baseline 0.012), median |∇_u V| 3.1 in the band |V| < 2 vs 0.13 elsewhere.
- T_ℓ = 3: all states get V < 0: the per-step hazard softplus(−ℓ/T) ≈ 0.1 summed over 25 steps; larger T_ℓ needs a
  margin offset (log(1 − d) = −softplus(−(ℓ − b)/T)) or a fitted calibration, not the temperature alone.
The held-out scan (6 checkpoints, all oracle decision points) runs after the sweep with the chosen config.
