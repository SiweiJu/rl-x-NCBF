### API (what d-train calls)
**Setup.**
- `spec = SV.ImaginationSpec.from_env(env, xi_active=meta["schema"]["xi_active"])`: layouts, the policy-observation function, margin configs, the maximum delay, and the P5 hook `collision_margin_fn`.
- `inputs = SV.inputs_from_env_state(env, env_state, spec)` builds a `ShieldInputs` with:
  - x0 = x⁺_t
  - xi_full, delay_buffer, delay_steps, command
  - first_step = episode_step == 0
  - stale = first_step or a shock / DR step happened just before (F13)
  - extra

**Value function.**
- `vf = SV.make_safety_value_fn(sv_cfg, spec, wm=wm, policy_fn=SV.make_imagined_policy(model.policy.apply, model.get_processed_action), policy_grad=False)`
- Call: `vf(params, inputs, u)` returns `(V (N,E), {"logp","min_margin"})`, with `params = {"wm": bundle, "policy": θ}`.
- All params are stop_gradient'ed. `policy_grad=True` (D2) leaves θ differentiable but still stops ψ.

**Shield.**
- `shield_fn = WS.make_shield_fn(sh_cfg, vf, nr_events=E)`
- Call: `shield_fn(params, inputs, u0, v_prev, strength=1.0)` returns `(u, v_exec, diag)`.
  - u = u0 + strength·Δu.
  - v_exec is V at the executed u. It is set to γ_c on stale rows and stored as the next v_prev.
  - The first step uses V^prev = γ_c internally.
  - diag holds the per-env values: active, delta_u(_norm), G_before, G_after, V, V_after, v_prev, gate_mask, grad_norm, nr_projections, stale, v0, logp, min_margin.
- The class form `WS.Shield.apply(params, inputs, u0, state, strength)` is the same.
- u is differentiable w.r.t. u0, including second order through a and c.

Formulas, as in plan §4.5–4.6:
- log(1−d_e) = −softplus(−(ℓ_e−b_e)/T_ℓ), log P̂_e = Σ_{j=1..H}, V_e = log P̂ − log1mexp(log P̂). The log1mexp has safe branches, so there are no NaN gradients at either end.
- g_e = V_e − (1−α̃)V_e^prev − α̃γ_c, G = −τ log Σ_e exp(−g_e/τ).
- a = ∇_u G(u⁰), u* = u⁰ + [−G]_+/‖a‖² · a. This is paper eq. 13 with hard projection and c = aᵀu⁰ − G.
- The total correction is capped at max_delta_u.
- Relinearization: G and ∇G are recomputed at u*; if G is still < 0, the projection is repeated, with the cap applied relative to u⁰.

Gated mode, step by step:
1. G(u⁰) is computed for all N envs.
2. The k = ⌈0.25 N⌉ lowest G among the non-stale envs are selected; these are the only envs that get the VJP, the relinearization and the post-check.
3. The other envs keep u⁰.

### Findings
1. **The shield works as intended mechanically.**
   - It intervenes exactly where G(u⁰) < 0: 2.1 % of env steps at p = 0.4 and 7.7 % at p = 0.8.
   - 97 % of interventions increase G.
   - Every violating env was inside the 25 % gate, so the gated and full shields give the same statistics.
   - bf16 changes nothing measurable.
2. **max_delta_u = 0.5 binds on almost every intervention.**
   - The mean ‖Δu‖ of active envs is 0.45–0.46.
   - Only 10–13 % of interventions reach G ≥ 0 (relin 1), and 6 % with relin 0.
   - The noise here is huge (std 1.0 per joint, ‖noise‖ ≈ 3.5), so the cap cannot undo it. Without the cap the
   corrections average ‖Δu‖ ≈ 4.1–4.3, about the size of the noise. They cut the fall rate from 0.606 to 0.345
   (p = 0.4) and from 0.981 to 0.819 (p = 0.8). Even then only a third of the interventions reach G ≥ 0 after one
   relinearization: G is strongly nonlinear over corrections that large.
3. **Sanity signal on falls (mode B, 1024 envs, binomial SE ≈ 1.5 pp).**
   - p = 0.4: the first-episode fall rate drops from 0.606 to 0.495 with the gated f32 shield (−11 pp); the four shield variants range from 0.489 to 0.519.
   - p = 0.8: 0.981 → 0.946–0.962.
   - Run-to-run spread of the no-shield baseline at the same seed was 0.970–0.981 at p = 0.8 (MJX on the GPU is not bit-deterministic).
   - This is not a method result: the policy was never trained with this shield, and the cap limits the corrections.
     Uncapped, the drops are much larger (see the cap table).
4. **Speed.** The plan default (gated, relin 1, post-check) is 5.1× a PPO env step (bf16), against the 2.5× target that T13 measured for the bare gated VJP. relin 0 is 3.5×. In a training iteration this adds about 128 × 22 ms ≈ 2.8 s per iteration, against 2.83 s for T06b PPO.

## Open questions / for the next step
- **Defaults to decide (d-train / main)**, all currently plan values:
  - relinearize 1 vs 0. Relin 1 resolves about 2× more interventions (10 % vs 6 % at cap 0.5) but costs +7 ms per step.
  - post_check. Turning it off saves one gated forward (~1.5 ms). V^prev then comes from the last linearization point instead of the executed action.
  - max_delta_u. The 0.5 cap is tight for these disturbances; the right check is the distribution of required corrections under the training policy's own sampling std.
- **The fall margin at eval.** T06b's height threshold is 0 at curriculum 1, so "fall" means IMU below the ground or the qvel limit. During training the threshold is (1−coeff)·0.8·h_nom, and the margins read the coefficient from ξ, so the shield follows the curriculum automatically. `fall_physical` is available as a second event.
- **Delay in imagination.** It uses `delay_steps_from_xi`: the fixed delay, or the worst case max when the delay is mixed. The env's per-step random draw is not reproduced. `delay_mode="given"` takes a supplied delay.
- **Not yet run on GPU:**
  - `mjx_oracle` (code is written; it needs `record_wm_state` and differentiates through mjx.step).
  - `mjx_nominal_1step` (not implemented).
  - The critic source through this shield (the existing ncbf.py shield remains the critic path).
- **Speed ideas, not done:**
  - Fuse the gating forward with the subset forward instead of recomputing the subset (saves ~1 forward of k envs).
  - Skip the relinearization VJP when no gated env is still violated (`lax.cond`).
  - Reuse T13's measurement in-scan inside d-train's loop.

### Action units (for judging ‖Δu‖)
The shield acts on the processed policy action u (`get_processed_action`; no clipping in T06b), and the PD target
of each joint is `nominal + u · scaling_factor + position_offset` (`control_functions/pd.py`). For Go2,
scaling_factor = 0.3 rad per action unit (`robots/unitree_go2/robot_config.py`). So, over the 12 joints:

| ‖Δu‖ (action units) | ‖Δ PD target‖ (L2) | ≈ per-joint RMS |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 (the cap) | 0.15 rad | 0.043 rad (2.5°) |
| 4.2 (uncapped corrections in the check) | 1.26 rad | 0.36 rad (21°) |

The eval noise std 1.0 per joint means 0.3 rad per joint on the PD targets.

### Open bug: NaN gradients through the shield under `lax.cond`
This is d-train's report. I reproduced it on the toy WM (`tests/test_wm_shield.py::_setup`, n = 32):
- `jax.grad` of Σ‖u − shield(u)‖² is finite when called plainly, and also when multiplied by 0.
- It is NaN on 28 / 32 rows when the same function sits inside `jax.lax.cond(True, f, g)`, for every config tried, including `gate="full"`, relin 0, no post-check.
- So it is not the gating, the projection or the relinearization. The likely cause is a ±inf local derivative somewhere in the value path that normally gets a *symbolic* zero cotangent; under cond the cotangents are instantiated as numeric zeros, and 0·inf = NaN. Candidates: the unused `tilt` term (`sqrt` of roll² + pitch²) and the `qvel` max inside `wm_state.margins`, or `nan_to_num` / `clip` in the policy observation.
- d-train has a workaround (`row_safe_vjp` + a skip-step guard), so this is not blocking.
