**Return vs episode length (job 179851, ours-42 and PPO-1042, 10 episodes per point).**

| horizon | ours p=0 return | PPO p=0 return | ours p=0.6 return | ours p=0.6 survive ≥250 steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 10.0 | 10.7 | 0.42 | 0.70 |
| 10 s | 16.3 (18.8 in an earlier run) | 20.4 | 0.41 | 0.50 |
| 20 s | 36.4 | 40.1 | 0.41 | 0.50 |
| paper | 7.85 | 7.20 | 0.337 | 0.84 |

- Return grows linearly at about 0.04 per step. `test()` simply sums the env rewards. Each reward term is already multiplied by dt; the total is clipped at 0 and then the alive term (0.001 per step) is added. There is no extra normalisation.
- The paper's returns therefore imply a horizon of about 4–5 s: 7.85 at p=0 and 0.337 at p=0.6, where only the alive term remains. The 250-step success threshold suggests 5 s, and **G0 part 1 uses EP_SECONDS=5**.
- A shorter horizon cannot explain the success gaps: survival to step 250 does not depend on the horizon.
- The command limit (`clip_max_velocity` 2.0 vs 1.0) has no effect: for Go2, max command speed = min(robot size × 2, clip) < 1.
- Returns are not deterministic across runs. The eval agent found the cause (T04 §7): the command is not resampled at reset, so episode 1 of each process starts from a zero command.
- calib2 (horizons 3/4/5 s with 30 episodes) failed once: `episode_length_in_seconds` is an int flag. I then cancelled it in favour of calib3, because the length question was already settled.

## G0 part 1: paper checkpoints + reconstructed eval vs paper figure
- **Setup:** the original wandb checkpoints of Fig. 2, 5 per method: PPO = `ncbf-q-no-safe` 1042…1442, NCBF = H=1 seeds 42/43/44/145/245, ours = H=25 same seeds.
- **Eval:** all 4 test settings at the paper's x values; committed semantics (noise std 1.0, perturbation 2×), EP 5 s, γ_c 0.405, eval seed 1, 10 episodes per pkl.
- **Runs:** array 179906 (tasks 0–6) and then 180177 (15 tasks, 2 parallel processes each), at most 2 concurrent; 330 pkls in `/home/ju/repo/pncbf/paper_eval_g0/ep5/`.
- **Comparison:** `paper_eval/compare_g0.py` aggregates exactly like `plot_line_plot_one_figure.py` (entry means, then mean and std over entries) and compares with `paper_curves.csv`.
- **Units:** z = (repro − paper) / max(paper SE, repro SE, 0.01), where paper SE = band = std/√5.
- **Output:** `paper_eval/results/g0_part1_ep5.{csv,txt}` (all 132 points).

| method | success: \|z\|≤1 / \|z\|≤2, median z, mean Δ | return: \|z\|≤2, median z |
|---|---|---|
| PPO-RL | 0 % / 0 %, **+7.7**, +0.44 | 9 %, +15.3 |
| PPO-RL-NCBF (H=1) | **55 % / 91 %**, +0.2, +0.03 | 32 %, +3.5 |
| ours (H=25) | 32 % / 68 %, **−1.7**, −0.14 | 27 %, +0.9 |

- **NCBF (H=1):** reproduces the paper within sampling error on 20 of 22 success points, so the eval reconstruction is right for this method.
- **Ours:** has the paper's shape under action noise, but sits 1–2 SE low. It is clearly worse under perturbation: flat 0.42–0.76 vs 0.70–0.96, heightfield z down to −8.6 at p=0.01.
- **PPO-RL:** is far too robust in the reconstruction. Under noise p=0.2 it gets 0.98 vs 0.10 (flat) and 0.86 vs 0.03 (hfield); under perturbation p=0.02 it gets 0.82 vs 0.37.
- **Consequence for Fig. 2:** the reconstruction does not reproduce the method ordering. With the committed eval, ours ≤ NCBF ≈ PPO under action noise.
- **Returns:** all about +1–2 above the paper. The likely cause is the 5 s horizon combined with the zero first command; the plan is to compare success only.
- **Conclusion:** the eval reconstruction is correct for the NCBF entries. The paper's PPO-RL points, and to a lesser degree the ours points, were produced with eval settings or code that differ from the committed `test()`. Candidates: stronger noise for PPO (0.5 × range reproduces the PPO collapse), a different or uncommitted shield for ours, perturbation magnitude about 3× instead of 2×.
- Neither the laptop pkls nor the laptop code is available. **G0 cannot pass with the paper checkpoints, so a T05 retrain can only be judged against this reconstruction, not against the figure.** Recommendation: use `paper_eval/results/g0_part1_ep5.csv` (paper checkpoints, committed eval) as the reference for T05/T06, and keep the paper figure only as a qualitative target.
