### Design notes
- **xi dims** (from scratch): the dims that vary over an eval-mode reset of 256 envs (full DR ranges) plus
  `curriculum_coeff` (varies only in training). Stored in the bundle schema (`xi_active`).
- **Replay**: time-major ring (L=1000, N, ...) of x+, u~, u, xi (active dims), x-_{t+1}, x+_{t+1}, shock/DR flag,
  done, terminated, start_ok. ~1.8 GB at 1024 envs (Go2: S=53, A=12, 263-ish xi dims). Windows: env and start
  uniform over the filled part, never across the write head; weight 0 if `start_ok` is false at the start (episode step
  0 or right after a shock / DR step = stale IMU, as T13) or an episode ends before the last step; method A teacher
  forcing + no loss on the step after a shock (`WMConfig.mask_post_shock_step`).
- **Negative buffer**: after each rollout up to 256 (random) terminations of the new rollout; for each, the 32 ring
  rows ending at the terminating step are copied (rows before the data start get done=1/start_ok=0). Windows from
  segments end 0..16 steps before the termination. When empty, the negative share is drawn from the ring.
- **Normalizers**: running mean/std (Chan) over non-shock, non-stale rows of the first 100 iterations, then frozen.
  The WM trains during that phase too (the shield is off then: warm-start cap 5 % = 381 iterations at 1e9 steps).
- **Latest-data eval** (before the WM trains on the new rollout): `d/wm_latest_kstep_loss` (k_train windows from the
  new rollout), `d/wm_auc_replay` (WM along the recorded applied actions with method-A teacher forcing, score = min
  fall margin over 25 steps, cut at the episode end; label = terminated within 25 steps), `d/wm_auc_imagination`
  (the shield's V(x_t, u_t), closed loop through the deterministic policy, 25 steps). The warm-start gate uses the
  replay AUC by default: it measures the WM, while the imagination AUC also depends on the policy and on the label
  noise of the stochastic policy + pushes.
- **Shield in the rollout**: `u0` = the sampled (processed) action; `u = u0 + s * du`, s = wm_ready * fade.
  V^prev per env = d-shield's v_exec (gamma_c at first steps / stale rows). While s = 0 the shield is skipped
  (`lax.cond`, no cost).
- **Anticipation through the WM shield**: `0.5 mean ||mu - shield(mu)||^2` on the first 1024 samples of each PPO
  minibatch, through the shield's VJP into u0 = mu_theta(s) (second order through the gated 25-step VJP); psi and
  the imagined policy stop_gradient. The shield's VJP is wrapped in `row_safe_vjp` (rows with a non-finite
  gradient -> 0; see section 3). It is computed (and zeroed) during the warm start, because a `lax.cond` around it
  produced NaN gradients.
- **D2**: `-mean log P(s, mu_theta(s))` (d-shield's value fn with `policy_grad=True`, f32): gradient into theta
  through u0 and the imagined policy, none into psi; zeroed before the warm start ends. `d2noshield` variant =
  D2 with `shield_enabled=False` (the "no shield" ablation).
- **Checkpoint**: resume checkpoint gains `"d"` = WM params, Adam state, normalizers + running stats, wm_ready,
  switch update, AUC streak, WM update count. Not restored (documented in `_restore_resume_checkpoint`): replay
  ring, negative buffer, V^prev (refilled by the next rollouts). `models/wm_latest.msgpack` (T13 bundle format,
  `world_model_eval.load_bundle`) is written next to `latest.model` at every model save.
- **Eval** of D checkpoints: `PPO.load` switches `method` back to `default` (mode A = policy alone), unless
  `--algorithm.method=d` is given explicitly.

## 3. Tests (`JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_d_training.py -q`, 10 tests, ~25 min on CPU)
All 10 pass on the final code (8 in one run, the 2 touched by the last fix re-run: `anticipation`, `end_to_end`):
1. `test_ring_write_wraps_and_windows_respect_episode_and_stale_masks`: wrap, no window across the write head or
   an episode end (ending at the done step is allowed), none starting at a stale row.
2. `test_neg_buffer_keeps_segments_ending_at_terminations`: segments end at the terminating rows; rows before the
   data start unusable; windows end 0..4 steps before the termination.
3. `test_wm_update_decreases_loss_on_a_tiny_env`: real Go2 env (16 envs), records (last_action of x-_{t+1} = u_t),
   12 iterations x 10 updates: loss < 0.5 x initial.
4. `test_warm_start_switch_logic` (streak, min positives, NaN AUC, latch, 5 % cap), 5. `test_fade_out_schedule`,
6. `test_masked_auc_matches_numpy`.
7. `test_d2_gradient_reaches_policy_not_wm`: grad wrt policy params != 0, wrt WM params == 0; 0 before warm start.
8. `test_anticipation_through_wm_shield_is_differentiable`: finite loss and gradients with V^prev = gamma_c (most
   rows uncorrected) and V^prev = 30 (all corrected).
9. `test_save_resume_restores_wm_state`: resume checkpoint round trip of the "d" state.
10. `test_tiny_end_to_end_d_run_without_nan`: 16 envs, 4 iterations, warm start switches on, shield acts, every
    logged metric finite, `wm_latest.msgpack` loads with `load_bundle`, the checkpoint loads as a plain policy.

Bugs found on the way:
- `wm_shield.cap_correction`: NaN gradient at zero correction (norm of 0) -> fixed by d-shield (4c72d47, my repro).
- Remaining NaN in the shield VJP on uncorrected rows of real WM data when the shield sits inside `lax.cond` or runs
  with strength 0 (forward finite; some local derivative is inf). Reported to d-shield with the captured batch
  (`scratchpad/cap.pkl`). Worked around here: no `lax.cond` in differentiated D terms, `row_safe_vjp` (non-finite
  gradient rows -> 0) around the shield in the anticipation loss, and a guard that skips a minibatch's policy/value
  step if its gradient is non-finite (`d/nonfinite_ppo_grad_rate`, D only).
